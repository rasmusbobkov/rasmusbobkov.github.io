### Proposed convergence and coverage procedure

At invocation start, the coordinator records effective rule/version, phase, current bundle/hashes, required roles and standards, scope and context exclusions, invocation identity, inner round and outer usage, remaining allowance and actual launch authority. A future rule applies only after separately authorized integration and instruction alignment; an in-flight invocation retains its starting rule and counters.

Critique retains `design_critic.md`, `structural.md`, `style.md`; design and structural apply `feature-spec-standard.md`. Readiness retains `implementation_readiness.md`, `codebase_details.md`, `style.md`; readiness and codebase apply `implementation-readiness-standard.md`. Style applies its persona. Each role completes independently with actual reads, current matching inputs, attributed findings and status. Exactly one assigned readiness reviewer analyzes the same pinned spec/plan/tasks in a distinct section of its report; other roles do not repeat it.

Aggregate results before repairs: all required roles completed, current coverage, zero unresolved Blocker/Major and no other outstanding stop yields one clean aggregate. Nits retain reviewer, scope and disposition without automatic confirmation. A missing/failed/unavailable/stale reviewer does not contribute zero findings. Preserve one retry for a failed reviewer; after a second failure record not run and stop affected clearance. Author self-review cannot fill the missing role.

A failed result stays failed. Author repair/disposition is not independent closure. Repin the bundle and delta, obtain independent affected verification on decisions/findings/scenarios/explanations, and obtain each retained unaffected reviewer's explicit applicability confirmation to those current inputs. An affected-only review cannot fill other roles. A later aggregate may converge at its first complete clean state. Material change invalidates affected coverage and earlier phase judgments; uncertain impact broadens review or stops for reconciliation. Recurring fixed findings reopen. Contests need permitted-input counter-evidence and independent agreement; disagreement stays open. Accepted-risk labels cannot waive required conditions.

Extra review must resolve a named high-consequence uncertainty or conflicting finding, not provide generic confidence. Its record includes evidence, deciding question, affected scope, current inputs, independent reviewer, expected signal, finite allowance and stopping condition. An unresolved question remains a stop. Use only actual remaining authority; scope changes, sensitive resources or exhausted caps still require their own decision.

Only the next genuinely input-ready phase is assigned; no empty reservations. Preserve stage reports and original findings.

Critique/readiness retain five inner rounds per invocation and one outer stage re-audit after repair. Record invocation/round/outer counters separately. Repair-verification rounds consume their applicable allowance; calling one a check does not avoid accounting. Comprehension has no inner loop: initial plus one ordinary re-audit, with any additional existing finite exception separately recorded. Churn remains a new finding at an applied fix's scope that reverting the fix would resolve. Churn/exhaustion/missing required review stops. A complete clean fifth round can converge only prospectively under the new rule with no other stop; a nonclean fifth stops. A new invocation, outer re-audit or drafting cannot conceal a sixth round, reset counters or transfer unused slots. Numerical budget is not launch authority.

This change's planning retains the current two-consecutive-clean requirement: a lone clean fifth round cannot clear it. No retrospective application is permitted. Preserve ordinary authorized first-mate continuation without routine extra approval questions, and preserve all actual product/scope/resource/cost/sensitive-access/exception/cap/churn/merge/deployment decisions.

### Alternatives considered

Its risk is readers following unchanged upstream text; explicit displacement and future fresh-session observation address, but do not prove away, that risk.

Keeping two-clean preserves the old confirmation but does not meet the accepted change. Splitting critique/readiness changes would temporarily create conflicting stopping rules. Reducing reviewer count or allowing author clearance would remove safeguards and is not accepted.
